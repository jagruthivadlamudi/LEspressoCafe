Coffee at L'Espresso

Espresso is the heart of our coffee menu. It is served in a small cup and has a rich and strong flavor. It is a great choice for customers who enjoy classic European coffee.

Our cappuccino combines espresso with steamed milk and milk foam. We also serve café lattes, which have more steamed milk and a smooth and creamy taste.

Customers can also order a macchiato, Americano, or mocha. Each drink is made differently, so customers can choose something depending on how strong or sweet they like their coffee.
