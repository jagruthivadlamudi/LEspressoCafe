About L'Espresso

I created L'Espresso because I wanted to build a website around something I genuinely enjoy—coffee, cafés, and the cozy atmosphere they bring. Cafés are places where people can slow down, have conversations, get work done, or simply enjoy a good cup of coffee, and I wanted my website to reflect that same feeling.

While designing this site, I focused on warm, neutral colors and a clean layout to create a simple but inviting look. The brown, cream, and beige palette was inspired by coffee and European café interiors. I chose those colors because they make the site feel comfortable, classic, and easy to navigate without making the design too busy.

This project also gave me the opportunity to strengthen my web development skills by working with multiple pages, consistent navigation, and an external stylesheet. I enjoyed combining a café theme with the HTML and CSS skills I am learning in this course.
